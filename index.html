Where teams get stuck

Three problems we hear most

  • Everyone is trying AI, but nothing is shared.

    People test tools on their own. Prompts, results and lessons never become a team practice.

  • Design is growing faster than its process.

    More people and more projects, with the same ad-hoc reviews and handoffs.

  • Leaders can't see what design is worth.

    Without a direction tied to metrics, design is hard to fund and easy to cut.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you build our product or website?

No. We help your team set direction, improve how design works, and adopt AI. Your team keeps building, and keeps the skills after we leave.

What does it cost?

Each step above shows a starting price. After the intro call we send a fixed quote for the step you choose.

Who do you work with?

Right now we take on nonprofits and organizations using AI for good. We work with design teams and the leaders they report to.

Do you work in person?

We're based in Toronto. Workshops can be in person in the Toronto area or remote. Assessments and partnerships work well remotely.

Why don't you list your names?

We keep Treehouse separate from our other professional work. You'll get our names, LinkedIn profiles and full backgrounds on the intro call, before you commit to anything.

What happens after I book a call?

We talk for 30 minutes about how your team works today. If we're a good fit, we suggest a first step. If we're not, we'll tell you.
